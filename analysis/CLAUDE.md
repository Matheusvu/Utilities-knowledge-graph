# analysis/ — reports, memos, the credit read

Where validated data becomes decision-useful work product.

- `curtailment/REPORT.md` — the curtailment monitor output: volumes by reason (reliability vs energetic) / region / asset, the compensation status, and the **credit read** (revenue-at-risk for financed renewable SPVs).
- `memos/` — issuer credit memos / one-pagers.
- `templates/` — memo and deck templates.

**Rule:** every figure must trace to a `processed/` cell (and onward to a `raw/` source). No numbers without lineage. Connect findings back to the credit lens: revenue-at-risk, cash-flow stability, debt-service capacity.
